# Audio credits — retain with every distributed game build

Audio is edited from the recordings/compositions credited below. Changes: selected short extracts, removal of excess boundary silence in effects, mono downmix in effects, level adjustment with headroom, small edge fades; selected long beds use end/start crossblending; converted/re-encoded to OGG Vorbis. Original creators do not endorse this game. Full per-file mappings and original download provenance: docs/AUDIO_ASSETS_LICENSES.md in the project distribution.
